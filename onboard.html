Cap Fahmy coaching agreement

Ahmed Fahmy (“Cap Fahmy”) provides fitness coaching and exercise programming. This is not medical advice, physiotherapy, diagnosis, or treatment.

If you experience red-flag symptoms (for example: unexplained chest pain, dizziness, sudden severe pain, neurological symptoms, or other concerning signs), stop training and seek appropriate medical care. Cap Fahmy will refer out of scope issues.

By signing, you confirm that:

  • You are voluntarily participating in a fitness coaching program.
  • You understand training involves physical risk and you accept responsibility for your participation.
  • You will disclose relevant health history truthfully on the intake form.
  • You understand Cap Fahmy coaches within fitness scope and may decline or pause coaching when referral is appropriate.
